Teams (svetra 0.1)
roles admin; member(Team)
auth email; signup open; confirm email; password 10
Team: id uuid pk; name text!
Task: id uuid pk; team ref(Team)!; author user!; title text! | index (team); (author)
policy members_see_team: Team select authenticated when has_role('member', id)
policy admins_see_teams: Team select authenticated when has_role('admin')
policy members_see_tasks: Task select authenticated when has_role('member', team)
policy members_add_tasks: Task insert authenticated when has_role('member', team) and author == auth.user
policy authors_edit_tasks: Task update,delete authenticated when author == auth.user and has_role('member', team)
hole start_team: (name Team.name) -> Team; writes Team; grants member
hole add_member: (team Team.id, person uuid) -> none; grants member; when has_role('member', team)
