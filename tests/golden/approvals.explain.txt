Approvals

What your app stores
  Docs
    - the signed-in person who owns it (required)
    - reviewer: a signed-in person (required)
    - title: text (required)
    - status: one of draft, submitted, approved or rejected (required) (starts as draft)

Signing in
  - People sign in with email (a password or an emailed link).
  - Anyone can create an account.
  - People confirm their email address before they can sign in.
  - Passwords need at least 10 characters.

Rules that always hold
  - A doc's author and reviewer are different.

Who can see and change what
  Docs
    - Signed-in people can see a doc when its author is them.
    - Signed-in people can see a doc when its reviewer is them and its status is not draft.
    - Signed-in people can create a doc when its author is them and its status is draft.
    - Signed-in people can change a doc when its author is them and its status is draft. After the change, its author is them and its status is draft or submitted.
    - Signed-in people can change a doc when its reviewer is them and its status is submitted. After the change, its reviewer is them and its status is approved or rejected. They can change only its status.
    - Signed-in people can delete a doc when its author is them and its status is draft.
    - Nobody can see docs any other way.

Before going live
  - Set up your own email sender (SMTP). Supabase's built-in sender is for testing and only sends a few emails an hour.
  - Set your app's real web address in Supabase, for sign-in links and the page people return to after signing in.
