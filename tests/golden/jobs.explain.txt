Invites

What your app stores
  Invites
    - the signed-in person who owns it (required)
    - email: text (required)
    - status: one of pending, accepted or expired (starts as pending)
    - expires at: a date and time (required)

Signing in
  - People sign in with email (a password or an emailed link).
  - Anyone can create an account.
  - People confirm their email address before they can sign in.
  - Passwords need at least 10 characters.

Who can see and change what
  Invites
    - Signed-in people can see an invite when its owner is them.
    - Signed-in people can create an invite when its owner is them.
    - Nobody can see invites any other way.
    - Nobody can change or delete invites directly. Only custom code can change them: expire_invites.

Custom code
  expire_invites
    - runs by itself every day at 03:00 (UTC). Nobody can run it from the app.
    - can read: invites. Can change: invites.

Before going live
  - Set up your own email sender (SMTP). Supabase's built-in sender is for testing and only sends a few emails an hour.
  - Set your app's real web address in Supabase, for sign-in links and the page people return to after signing in.
  - Code that runs by itself on a schedule needs Supabase's pg_cron extension. The update turns it on; if your project refuses, switch it on under Database, Extensions in the Supabase dashboard.
