Clinic

What your app stores
  Visits
    - patient: a signed-in person (required)
    - notes: text

Roles
  - admin: runs the clinic (needs a second factor)

Signing in
  - People sign in with email (a password or an emailed link) or Google.
  - Only people you invite can create an account.
  - People confirm their email address before they can sign in.
  - Passwords need at least 12 characters, with lowercase and uppercase letters and digits.
  - The admin role only counts after a second factor, an authenticator app.

Who can see and change what
  Visits
    - Signed-in people can see and create a visit when its patient is them.
    - People with the admin role can see, change and delete visits.
    - Nobody can see visits any other way.

Before going live
  - Create a Google sign-in app, then set SUPABASE_AUTH_EXTERNAL_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID and SUPABASE_AUTH_EXTERNAL_GOOGLE_SECRET.
  - Set up your own email sender (SMTP). Supabase's built-in sender is for testing and only sends a few emails an hour.
  - Set your app's real web address in Supabase, for sign-in links and the page people return to after signing in.
