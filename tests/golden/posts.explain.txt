App

What your app stores
  Audit logs
    - note: text
  Posts
    - the signed-in person who owns it (required)
    - body: text

Who can see and change what
  Audit logs
    - Audit logs are private: the app can't reach them at all. Only custom code can change them: write_audit.
  Posts
    - Signed-in people can see, create, change and delete a post when its author is them.
    - People who aren't signed in can see posts.
    - Nobody can see posts any other way.

Custom code
  write_audit
    - returns: nothing
    - can change: audit logs.

Worth knowing
  - People who aren't signed in can reach some posts that signed-in people may not be able to. Signing in could take access away; if posts should be public, make them public for everyone.
