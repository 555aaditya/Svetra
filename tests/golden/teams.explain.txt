Teams

What your app stores
  Teams
    - name: text (required)
  Tasks
    - the team it belongs to (required)
    - the signed-in person who owns it (required)
    - title: text (required)

Roles
  - admin: runs the service
  - member: belongs to one team (held for one team at a time)

Signing in
  - People sign in with email (a password or an emailed link).
  - Anyone can create an account.
  - People confirm their email address before they can sign in.
  - Passwords need at least 10 characters.

Who can see and change what
  Teams
    - People with the member role for a team can see that team.
    - People with the admin role can see teams.
    - Nobody can see teams any other way.
    - Nobody can create, change or delete teams directly. Only custom code can change them: start_team.
  Tasks
    - People with the member role for a task's team can see that task.
    - Signed-in people can create a task when they have the member role for its team and its author is them.
    - Signed-in people can change and delete a task when its author is them and they have the member role for its team.
    - Nobody can see tasks any other way.

Custom code
  start_team
    - takes: name (text)
    - returns: a team
    - who can call it: signed-in people
    - can change: teams.
    - can give people the member role, or take it away, and no other role.
  add_member
    - takes: team (a team), person (an id)
    - returns: nothing
    - who can call it: signed-in people, only when they have the member role for the team
    - can't read or change any data
    - can give people the member role, or take it away, and no other role.

Before going live
  - Set up your own email sender (SMTP). Supabase's built-in sender is for testing and only sends a few emails an hour.
  - Set your app's real web address in Supabase, for sign-in links and the page people return to after signing in.
