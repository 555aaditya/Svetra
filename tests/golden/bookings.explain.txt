Bookings

What your app stores
  Locations
    - name: text (required)
  Bookings
    - customer: a signed-in person (required)
    - the location it belongs to (required)
    - starts: a date and time (required)

Roles
  - admin: runs the business
  - staff: works at one location (held for one location at a time)

Who can see and change what
  Locations
    - People with the staff role for a location can see that location.
    - People with the admin role can see, create and change locations.
    - Nobody can see locations any other way.
    - Nobody can delete locations directly.
  Bookings
    - Signed-in people can see and create a booking when its customer is them.
    - People with the staff role for a booking's location can see and change that booking.
    - People with the admin role can see, change and delete bookings.
    - Nobody can see bookings any other way.
